# 3D Baseball Game

**3D sports game · Unity · 2026 · Client project, in development**

A full 3D baseball game with batting, pitching and fielding, AI-controlled teams, and a broadcast-style camera that follows every play from the pitch to the final out.

## What I built

- **Batting and hit detection**: timing-based swings, contact detection, and ball flight after the hit
- **Switch hitters**: batters step into the box on the side opposite the pitcher's throwing arm
- **Pitcher and catcher logic**: pitch delivery, pitch placement, and the catcher's role in each play
- **Fielding AI**: fielders read the ball, chase it down, and throw to the right base for force outs and tags
- **AI vs AI matches**: both teams can be controlled by AI for full simulated games
- **Dynamic camera system**: the camera follows the ball and the action, switching between batter, fielders, and runners
- **Playtest-driven milestones**: features delivered in milestones, with QA reports from playtests guiding each round of fixes

## Tech stack
